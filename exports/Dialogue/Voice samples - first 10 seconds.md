# Voice samples: first 10 seconds

Speech blocks for the voice tool in your screenshots, one block per line. Export each result as WAV, name it as shown, and put it in `assets/vo/`. The video then plays it on that line.

## Settings for every block

- **Speaker:** Speaker 1 – Laomedeia. Keep this voice for all of Lisa's lines.
- **Style:** choose the custom option ("Describe the voice style") and paste the style text given in each block. If you would rather use a preset, pick **Friendly**. Avoid **Promote**, whose long vowels make lines run past their slot, and **Narration**, whose dramatic pauses do the same.
- **Expressions:** use only `<short pause>`, and only where it appears in the text below. These lines don't need laughs or chuckles.
- **Spelling:** the respelled words (Tuh-nahng, L-P-A, doh-nee, May Ling) are there only for pronunciation. If the voice already says Tenang, LPA, donee and Mei Ling correctly, use the normal spelling.
