The script

Twenty-five residents: an opening, two questions, and for the thirteen without an LPA a closing pitch of three arguments, one of which lands. The arguments are the part to test on real people.

The art

Terrain, buildings, interiors and characters are all drawn from coloured blocks and pixel maps — no image files. What is missing is walk-cycle frames, so everyone still slides rather than steps.

The ending

What happens when all twenty-five are signed — and what happens if some are not. The years pass, and the kampong is shown again. That is where the hundred-person grid would come in.
