The Rules

The clock

  • • Monday 06:00 to Sunday 09:00. One day is six real minutes.
  • • Runs while walking. Pauses in dialogue, the Notebook and cutscenes. Reading is never punished.
  • • A Backfire costs 30 minutes and the neighbour cools off until the next phase.

The Doubts

  • • Three Doubts per neighbour, cleared across three encounters in different places and phases.
  • • Each correct reply unlocks one of 30 Fact Cards, every one cited to the Mental Capacity Act.
  • • Already hold the next card? Skip straight to the next encounter. Learning from one neighbour speeds up the next.

The goal

  • • Twelve residents booked for Legacy Day by Saturday night. Convinced neighbours pin their names on the CC noticeboard.
  • • Bronze 6 · Silver 9 · Gold 12 · Platinum 12 with zero walk-offs.
  • • No game over. The roadshow happens with whoever came. The rest get an epilogue card.

Wrong answers are never punished with a game over. They are punished with time, which is the whole lesson.
