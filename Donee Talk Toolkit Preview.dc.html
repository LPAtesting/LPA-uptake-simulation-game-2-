1Ask Lisa: Donee Talk, a conversation toolkit for donors

DoneeTalkToolkit.dc.html is one page that fits iPhone and Android phones and switches to the desktop layout at 900px and wider. Lisa takes donors through the conversation starters, replies to common concerns and the guiding principles word for word, each with a Copy line button; the whole toolkit is under Toolkit. Typed messages such as "my brother says he is too busy" are matched to the closest line, and questions unrelated to asking a donee are politely turned away.
