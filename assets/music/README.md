# LPA GO! music

31 loops and 9 stings, 16-bit stereo WAV at 24000 Hz, rendered from lpa-go-music.js.
Loops are cut to loop seamlessly (the title theme loops from bar 3).

Copied from the LPA GO Music repo (LPAtesting/LPA-GO-Music). The game plays them through lpa-go-soundtrack.js: it picks the loop for the moment each frame, crossfades between them and plays the stings over a ducked loop. The ♪ button in the header turns the music off and on. To change a track, replace its file here and keep the name.
