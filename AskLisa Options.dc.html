1AskLisa: three ways to talk to Lisa at home

All three are live. Lisa keeps her own routine (waters the money plant, takes a tea break in the armchair, reads, dozes off) and drops it to come over when you start typing. Answers come from Claude, limited to the LPA GO! Fact Bank, and each one unlocks the Fact Cards it cites; without the model she quotes the cards directly. Tap Lisa to poke her, or tap the plant, armchair, lamp or clock. In the intro video she is about 31% of the frame height; here she is 33 to 55%.

1aTalking-Tom room

Full-bleed room, Lisa at 4× (44% of the screen). Speech bubble over her head, follow-up questions above the input, and Talking-Tom side buttons to send her off to water, make tea or read.

1bRoom above, chat below

Lisa at 3× in the top half (33% of the screen); a game-style dialogue panel holds the whole conversation and cited Fact Cards. Best for longer reading and scrolling back.

1cClose-up, RPG dialogue

Camera at 5× follows her around the room (55% of the screen). Answers type out in an LPA GO! dialogue box, then two follow-up questions appear as a choice menu, like the game's replies.
